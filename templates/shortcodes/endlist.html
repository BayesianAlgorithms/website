{#- Lets a list item continue with text after a nested list, without wrapping every item in a paragraph tag. In Markdown, the only way to end a nested list before more text in the outer item is a blank line, but that makes the outer list loose, and CommonMark then paragraph-wraps all of its items. An HTML comment also ends the inner list, since it can interrupt a paragraph, while keeping the list tight; the minifier removes it from the output. The rendered output must be exactly the comment, with no newline on either side, which the surrounding whitespace control ensures. -#}{#- -#}
